## Honey and soy roast thighs

The first thing you cooked. Sticky, salty, sweet, with greens.

*60 minutes · Low effort · one portion · cooks from frozen*

### Ingredients

- 2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 1 generous pinch per thigh flaky salt *(doesn't scale with portions)*
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tsp frozen chopped garlic
- 1 tbsp frozen chopped ginger
- 150 g tenderstem broccoli
- 2 pak choi
- 2 tbsp water, for the greens

### Method

**−0:15** — **Oven on.** Gas Mark 6, a full 15 minutes — gas ovens under-read early. Separate **2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs**; cold water over the block for a minute if they're stuck.

**0:00** — **Chicken in, bare.** **2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs** into the tin dry and bare, skin-side up, spaced apart, upper-middle shelf. No oil, no salt yet — neither sticks to frozen skin.

**0:10** — **Prep the greens.** Trim 1–2cm off the ends of **150 g tenderstem broccoli**; leave the stems whole. Halve **2 pak choi** lengthways and rinse the cut faces hard — grit collects at the base.

**0:30** — **Tip the water out.** Chicken out. Tip all the water out of the tin — frozen chicken sheds a lot and it poaches the underside. Rub the skin with **2 tsp olive oil** and **1 generous pinch per thigh flaky salt**. Turn the tin round, back in, up to Gas Mark 7. ⚠️

**0:40** — **Down to Gas 6, make the glaze.** Simmer **3 tbsp soy sauce**, **2 tbsp honey**, **1 tbsp rice vinegar**, **2 tsp frozen chopped garlic**, **1 tbsp frozen chopped ginger** and a grind of pepper for 5–6 minutes until it coats a spoon. Keep it moving so the honey doesn't catch.

**0:45** — **Half the glaze on.** Brush thinly. Back in for the last 10 minutes. Never roast with this on from the start — it'll be black before the chicken is done. ⚠️

**0:50** — **Second coat, greens on.** Rest of the glaze on the chicken. **150 g tenderstem broccoli** in a pan with **2 tbsp water, for the greens**, lid on, 4 minutes. **2 pak choi** cut-side down for the last 2. Drain hard.

**0:55** — **Chicken out.** 75°C at the thickest part away from the bone, or juices completely clear. Pink at the bone means 5 more minutes and check again.

**1:00** — **Rest and plate.** 5 minutes' rest. Greens down, chicken on top, tin juices spooned over.

### Worth knowing

- From thawed, drop about 20 minutes: 40–45 total, and oil and salt at the start instead of at 0:30.
- Make the glaze near the end. It stiffens as it cools and you'd have to loosen it with water.

## Miso butter roast thighs

Richer than the honey version. The same butter seasons the greens.

*60 minutes · Low effort · one portion · cooks from frozen*

### Ingredients

- 2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 1 generous pinch per thigh flaky salt *(doesn't scale with portions)*
- 30 g butter, softened
- 1 tbsp barley miso
- 2 tsp frozen chopped ginger
- 1 tsp frozen chopped garlic
- 150 g tenderstem broccoli
- 2 pak choi
- 60 g frozen chopped peppers
- 1 tbsp olive oil, for the greens
- 75 ml water, for the greens
- 1 tsp rice vinegar

### Method

**−0:15** — **Oven on, butter out.** Gas Mark 6, full 15 minutes. Separate **2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs**. Get **30 g butter, softened** out to soften and tip **2 tsp frozen chopped ginger** and **1 tsp frozen chopped garlic** into a saucer to thaw.

**0:00** — **Chicken in, bare.** **2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs** into the tin dry and bare, skin-side up, spaced, upper-middle shelf. No oil, no salt yet.

**0:05** — **Mash the miso butter.** **30 g butter, softened**, **1 tbsp barley miso**, **2 tsp frozen chopped ginger**, **1 tsp frozen chopped garlic** and a grind of pepper, mashed with a fork until evenly streaked. Leave at room temperature — it has to be spreadable at 0:45.

**0:15** — **Prep the greens.** Trim **150 g tenderstem broccoli**. Halve **2 pak choi** lengthways and rinse the cut faces hard.

**0:30** — **Tip the water out.** Chicken out. Tip all the water out of the tin. Rub the skin with **2 tsp olive oil** and **1 generous pinch per thigh flaky salt**. Turn the tin round, back in, up to Gas Mark 7. ⚠️

**0:40** — **Down to Gas Mark 5.** Not 6. Miso catches faster than a honey glaze, and the crisping is already done.

**0:45** — **Half the butter on.** Spread thinly with the back of a spoon. Back in for the last 10 minutes. Don't open the door again until 0:55. ⚠️

**0:47** — **Greens.** **60 g frozen chopped peppers** into a hot dry pan, 3 minutes until the water's hissed off. **1 tbsp olive oil, for the greens** and **150 g tenderstem broccoli**, 2 minutes. **75 ml water, for the greens** in, lid on, 3 minutes. **2 pak choi** cut-side down, lid on, 2 minutes.

**0:55** — **Chicken out.** 75°C at the thickest part away from the bone, or juices completely clear.

**0:57** — **Finish the greens.** Lid off, reduce until the liquid is glossy and just coats the stems — watch the pan, not the clock. Off the heat, stir in half the remaining butter, taste, then add the rest only if it needs it. Then **1 tsp rice vinegar**.

**1:00** — **Plate.** Greens in a bowl, chicken on top so the skin stays out of the liquid.

### Worth knowing

- No soy anywhere in this. The miso carries all the salt.
- If the butter melts to liquid while it waits, fridge for 15 minutes and stir it back together. Melted butter won't hold the miso and runs off the skin.
- The vinegar goes in off the heat at the very end — it cuts the fat, and boiling it off wastes it.

## Coconut Japanese curry

- 1 tbsp olive oil, for the sauce
- 1 tsp frozen chopped garlic
- 2 tsp frozen chopped ginger

- 1 tbsp sultanas (optional)
- 150 g tenderstem broccoli
- 2 pak choi

### Method

**−0:15** — **Oven on.** Gas Mark 6, full 15 minutes. Separate **2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs**.

**0:00** — **Chicken in, bare.** Dry and bare, skin-side up, spaced, upper-middle shelf. No glaze on this one at any point.

**0:40** — **Down to Gas 6, start the sauce.** **60 g frozen chopped peppers** into a hot dry pan, 3 minutes until the hissing stops. Then **1 tbsp olive oil, for the sauce**, 2 minutes. **1 tsp frozen chopped garlic** and **2 tsp frozen chopped ginger**, 30 seconds.

**0:45** — **Water and roux.** **175 ml water, for the sauce** in, bring to a simmer. Take the pan off the heat, break in **18 g Golden Curry roux**, stir until completely dissolved. Lumps won't come out later. ⚠️

**0:55** — **Chicken out.** 75°C at the thickest part away from the bone.

**1:00** — **Plate.** Sauce and greens in a bowl, chicken on top so the skin stays out of the liquid.

### Worth knowing

- No glaze and no soy. The roux is heavily salted and soy on top makes it inedible.
- No chilli — the roux is already medium hot. No cornflour — it thickens itself.
- Box ratio is 92g to 720ml for 5 servings, so one portion is 18g to about 145ml. 175ml here because there's no rice and you want enough sauce.
- Don't braise bone-in thighs in the roux. It catches on the bottom and you can't judge doneness in an opaque sauce.

## Gochujang chicken

Boneless thighs, browned then braised in their own sauce. Thirty minutes.

*30 minutes · Medium effort · one portion*

> **Not cooked yet.** Written for the two boneless thighs you defrosted, not yet cooked. The braise timing and the gochujang quantity are estimates — taste the sauce at the end before you commit to it.

### Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp sunflower oil
- 100 g frozen diced onions
- 1 tsp frozen chopped garlic
- 2 tsp frozen chopped ginger
- 1½ tbsp gochujang paste
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 150 ml water
- 10 g butter
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (optional)
- 150 g tenderstem broccoli
- 2 pak choi

### Method

**0:00** — **Brown the thighs.** Pat **2 boneless, skinless chicken thighs** dry and salt lightly. **1 tbsp sunflower oil** in the deep pan, medium-high. Thighs in flat, 4 minutes undisturbed until properly browned. Turn, 2 minutes. Out onto a plate — they won't be cooked through yet, and shouldn't be.

**0:06** — **Onions.** **100 g frozen diced onions** into the same pan, 5 minutes until soft and the water's gone.

**0:11** — **Aromatics.** **1 tsp frozen chopped garlic** and **2 tsp frozen chopped ginger**. 30 seconds.

**0:12** — **Paste off the heat.** Take the pan off the heat. **1½ tbsp gochujang paste** in, stir 20 seconds in the residual heat. It's glucose-syrup-first, so on a hot pan it catches and goes bitter — the same failure as the paprika in the braise. ⚠️

**0:13** — **Liquid.** **150 ml water**, **2 tsp soy sauce** and **1 tsp rice vinegar** in. Back on the heat, scrape the base, bring to a simmer.

**0:14** — **Braise.** Thighs back in with any juices from the plate. Lid on, low, 12 minutes, turning once. **150 g tenderstem broccoli** and **2 pak choi** in alongside for the last 6 if you're having them.

**0:26** — **Check and reduce.** Thighs out. 75°C at the thickest part, or cut it and look for no pink. Boil the sauce hard 2–3 minutes until it coats a spoon.

**0:29** — **Finish.** Off the heat. **10 g butter** in, swirl until glossy. **1 tsp toasted sesame oil (optional)** now. Taste — it shouldn't need more soy.

**0:30** — **Plate.** Slice the thighs thickly and put them back into the sauce.

### Worth knowing

- Boneless thighs have no bone to buffer them and no skin to protect them, so they can't take the 60-minute roast the other gochujang recipe uses. Browning then braising is what keeps them from drying out.
- Don't add honey. Shop gochujang lists glucose syrup before the miso paste.
- Gochujang keeps three weeks in the fridge once opened.

## Thai red curry with chicken

Boneless thighs in coconut and red curry paste. Thirty minutes, one pan.

*30 minutes · Low effort · one portion*

### Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp sunflower oil
- 100 g frozen diced onions
- 1 tsp frozen chopped garlic
- 2 tsp frozen chopped ginger
- 1½ tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 200 ml coconut milk
- 100 ml water
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp honey
- ½ lemon
- 60 g frozen spinach (optional)
- 150 g tenderstem broccoli
- 2 pak choi

### Method

**0:00** — **Dice and brown.** Cut **2 boneless, skinless chicken thighs** into rough 3cm pieces — they shrink, so don't go smaller. Pat dry, salt lightly. **1 tbsp sunflower oil** in the pan, high heat. Chicken in, 4 minutes without moving it, until browned on one side. Out onto a plate; it won't be cooked through.

**0:05** — **Onions.** **100 g frozen diced onions** into the same pan, 5 minutes until soft and the water's gone.

**0:10** — **Aromatics.** **1 tsp frozen chopped garlic** and **2 tsp frozen chopped ginger**. 30 seconds.

**0:11** — **Fry the paste.** **1½ tbsp Thai red curry paste** in, 1 minute, stirring. It'll smell sharp, then sweet. Frying the paste is what wakes it up — skip this and the whole thing tastes flat. ⚠️

**0:12** — **Liquid.** **200 ml coconut milk** and **100 ml water** in. Scrape the base. Bring to a simmer.

**0:13** — **Simmer the chicken.** Chicken back in with any juices from the plate. Lid on, low, 12 minutes. Thigh is forgiving — it gets more tender, not less, in a wet sauce.

**0:25** — **Greens.** **150 g tenderstem broccoli** in, lid on, 4 minutes. **2 pak choi** in for the last 2. **60 g frozen spinach (optional)** now if you're using it.

**0:29** — **Season.** Off the heat. **1 tbsp fish sauce**, **1 tsp honey**, then the juice of half a **½ lemon**. Taste between each — this is the whole dish. It'll want more fish sauce and more acid than feels right. ⚠️

**0:30** — **Serve.** Bowl and spoon. Check a thick piece is cooked through before you sit down.

### Worth knowing

- Boneless thighs need thawing — you can't dice them frozen. Fridge overnight is the reliable route. Cold water works but takes longer than it sounds: separate them under running cold water first (5–10 minutes), then a sealed bag in a bowl of cold water for 30–45 minutes, changing the water halfway. Cold only, never warm, and cook the same day.
- Aim for firm, not soft. Partly frozen meat dices better — the knife doesn't drag and the pieces stay square. If the centres are still icy, wait; don't cook it half-frozen and hope.
- Don't use bone-in skin-on for this. The skin goes flabby in sauce and bones are awkward in a bowl.
- The honey isn't sweetness for its own sake — it's balancing the salt and acid. Leave it out and taste before deciding you needed it.
- Half a tin of coconut milk left: fridge 3–4 days, or freeze it.

## Chicken katsu curry

The only one that needs real attention and a pan of hot oil.

*40 minutes · High effort · one portion*

### Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 3 tbsp plain flour, seasoned with salt and pepper
- 1 egg, beaten *(round up to a whole one)*
- 60 g panko breadcrumbs
- 1 cm depth in the pan sunflower oil *(doesn't scale with portions)*
- 1 tbsp sunflower oil, for the sauce
- 100 g frozen diced onions
- 1 tsp frozen chopped garlic
- 2 tsp frozen chopped ginger
- 300 ml water
- 36 g Golden Curry roux
- 150 g tenderstem broccoli
- 2 pak choi
- 2 tbsp water, for the greens

### Method

**0:00** — **Sauce first.** **1 tbsp sunflower oil, for the sauce** in a pan, **100 g frozen diced onions**, 8 minutes on medium until soft and colouring. **1 tsp frozen chopped garlic** and **2 tsp frozen chopped ginger**, 30 seconds. **300 ml water**, simmer 5 minutes. Off the heat, break in **36 g Golden Curry roux**, stir until dissolved. Back on low, 4 minutes. Set aside — it reheats fine.

**0:18** — **Three plates.** **3 tbsp plain flour, seasoned with salt and pepper** on one, **1 egg, beaten** on the second, **60 g panko breadcrumbs** on the third.

**0:20** — **Flatten the thighs.** Open **2 boneless, skinless chicken thighs** out flat and bash the thick parts with the base of a pan to an even 1.5cm. This is what stops the crumb burning before the middle cooks. ⚠️

**0:22** — **Coat.** Flour, shake off the excess. Egg, let it drip. Panko, pressed on firmly. Wet crumbs fall off in the pan.

**0:26** — **Fry.** **1 cm depth in the pan sunflower oil**, medium-high. Ready when a crumb sizzles steadily — roughly 170°C. 4–5 minutes a side, turned once. Go by colour, not the clock: deep golden brown, not pale. Still pale after a turn each means the oil is too cool — turn it up.

**0:28** — **Greens.** **150 g tenderstem broccoli** in a pan with **2 tbsp water, for the greens**, lid on, 4 minutes. **2 pak choi** for the last 2.

**0:36** — **Check and rest.** 75°C at the thickest part. Panko hides how far along the inside is. Rest on a plate, not kitchen paper, or the underside softens.

**0:40** — **Plate.** Slice before saucing, not after. Greens down, sliced katsu on top, sauce poured over the chicken.

### Worth knowing

- One egg coats two thighs comfortably. Don't scale it below one — beat a second in if you're doing four.
- Oil depth scales with pan diameter, not portions. A 28cm pan takes close to a litre at 1cm.
- Variation: swap 100ml of the sauce water for coconut milk. Rounder and sweeter — closer to a British takeaway katsu than a Japanese one.
- Used oil: cool completely, then strain back into a bottle through a sieve (good for 2–3 more fries), or pour into something disposable and bin it. Never down the sink.

## Chicken and chorizo braise

Worth doubling — it reheats better than anything else here.

*70 minutes · Medium effort · one portion*

### Ingredients

- 2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, thawed
- 75 g cooking chorizo, sliced 1cm thick
- 100 g frozen diced onions
- 1 tsp frozen chopped garlic
- 50 g frozen chopped peppers
- 1½ tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp tomato purée
- 200 ml water
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 150 g tenderstem broccoli
- 2 pak choi
- 2 tbsp water, for the greens

### Method

**0:00** — **Render the chorizo.** Pat **2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, thawed** dry, salt and pepper the skin. Deep pan, no oil, medium heat. **75 g cooking chorizo, sliced 1cm thick** in, 5 minutes until it's rendered a pool of orange fat and crisped at the edges. Lift it out with a slotted spoon; leave the fat.

**0:05** — **Brown the chicken.** Thighs skin-side down in that fat, 6–7 minutes undisturbed, until deep brown. Turn, 2 minutes. Out onto a plate.

**0:14** — **Onions and peppers.** Tip off all but 2 tbsp of the fat. **100 g frozen diced onions** in, 8 minutes until soft and browning. **1 tsp frozen chopped garlic** and **50 g frozen chopped peppers**, 3 minutes. Watch the peppers — anything scorched in the base ends up in the sauce when you deglaze. ⚠️

**0:25** — **Spices off the heat.** Take the pan off the heat. **1½ tsp smoked paprika** and **2 tsp tomato purée** in, stir 20 seconds in the residual heat, then the water immediately. Ground paprika burns fast in a hot pan and burnt paprika is distinctly bitter. ⚠️

**0:26** — **Deglaze.** **200 ml water** in. Scrape the bottom of the pan properly — that brown residue is most of the flavour.

**0:27** — **Braise.** Chorizo back in. Thighs back in skin-side up, sitting on top of the liquid rather than under it. Lid on, lowest heat that keeps it barely bubbling, 35 minutes. Check at 15 and 25; add 50ml water if it's drying out.

**1:02** — **Finish the sauce.** Chicken out, check 75°C. Lid off, boil the sauce hard until it coats a spoon. Off the heat, **1 tsp rice vinegar** in. Taste for salt — the chorizo has done most of it.

**1:05** — **Greens.** **150 g tenderstem broccoli** in a separate pan with **2 tbsp water, for the greens**, lid on, 5 minutes. **2 pak choi** for the last 3.

**1:10** — **Plate.** Chicken back into the sauce, greens alongside.

### Worth knowing

- Hob only. No pan here is oven-safe. If you want the oven, brown in the pan then tip into an oven dish, covered with foil, Gas Mark 4 for 35 minutes.
- Needs thawed chicken — you can't brown frozen skin. Fridge overnight.
- If you smell scorching, take the pan straight off the heat, don't stir, and pour the contents into another pan leaving what's stuck behind.

- 2 pak choi
- 2 tbsp water, for the greens

### Method

## Glazes at a glance

Swap any of these onto roast thighs or fish.

*6 minutes · Low effort · one portion*

### Method

**Honey and soy.** 3 tbsp soy, 2 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tsp garlic, 1 tbsp ginger, pepper. Simmer 5–6 minutes until it coats a spoon. Gas Mark 6, last 10 minutes.

**Miso butter.** 30g soft butter, 1 tbsp barley miso, 2 tsp ginger, 1 tsp garlic. No cooking. Gas Mark 5, last 10 minutes. Half on the meat, half stirred through the greens off the heat.

**Gochujang.** 2 tbsp gochujang, 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp garlic, ½ tsp fish sauce. No cooking. Gas Mark 5, last 10 minutes.

**Mustard butter.** 30g soft butter, 2 tsp Dijon, black pepper. Best on fish — 15 minutes at Gas Mark 5, and the glaze can go on from the start since fish cooks fast.

### Worth knowing

- Anything with sugar, honey or miso goes on in the last 10 minutes at Gas Mark 5. Never earlier, never hotter.
- Never stack two salty ferments. Miso and soy, or gochujang and soy at full strength, is salt on salt.

# This kitchen

**Oven marks.** Gas 4 = 180°C. Gas 5 = 190°C. Gas 6 = 200°C. Gas 7 = 220°C.

**Gas oven.** Hotter at the top, and humid, because burning gas produces water vapour. Crisping is harder than electric. Upper-middle shelf, turn the tin once.

**No oven-safe pans.** Every pan here is hob-only — plastic handles and lid knobs. Oven work goes in a dish or a tin.

**Chicken doneness.** 75°C at the thickest part, away from the bone. Or juices completely clear. Bone-in thighs are forgiving — five extra minutes won't dry them out.

**From frozen.** Safe provided it reaches temperature. Takes roughly 50% longer, and the skin never crisps as well.

**Frozen aromatics.** Loose, not cubes. 1 tsp garlic is about 1½ cloves. Level spoons, not heaped — heaped roughly doubles it.

**Frozen vegetables.** Wet. Dry-fry in a hot pan with no oil until the hissing stops, then add fat. Skip it and they steam.

**Loose lids.** Nothing has a tight lid except the deep frying pan. Add a minute to any lidded step, and expect more reduction while the lid is on.

# Where things live

**Fridge once opened.** Gochujang (3 weeks), Thai red curry paste (~6 weeks), tomato purée, anchovies under their oil (~2 weeks), Dijon, barley miso (months), creamed coconut wrapped tight (~2 weeks). Sesame oil is officially cupboard, but fridge it if you use it slowly.

**Cupboard.** Fish sauce — about a quarter salt, keeps a year plus. Plain flour, sunflower oil, unopened coconut tins, Golden Curry roux.

**Opened coconut milk.** Out of the tin, into the fridge, 3–4 days. Or freeze it.

**Cooked chicken.** Fridge within 2 hours, eat within 2 days, reheat once until piping hot. Oven at 190°C for 12–15 minutes uncovered beats the microwave. Cold off the bone is often better than either.

**Greens.** Pak choi 4–5 days, tenderstem about a week. Store unwashed.

**Raw chicken and fish.** Freezer on the day you buy it unless you're cooking within 48 hours. Check fish packs — much supermarket fish has been frozen and thawed already and can't be refrozen.

# Things that didn't work

- Honey or miso glaze on from the start — black before the chicken is done
- Frozen peppers in the roasting tin — they water the pan and steam the skin
- Skipping the tip-out at 0:30 — the biggest single cause of poor skin
- Soy on top of Golden Curry roux, or on top of miso — salt on salt
- Coconut in a vinegar-based sauce — splits the roundness, tastes sour and fatty at once
- Ground paprika into a hot pan — bitter, and it doesn't cook out
- Reheated cooked greens — grey and limp. Four minutes fresh instead.
